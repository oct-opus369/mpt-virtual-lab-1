Aim

Explore how a DC source delivers power to a variable load. Use the interactive circuit to observe current, load voltage, power and efficiency, then verify the maximum-power condition.

Open interactive simulationReview the theory

Work through the pretest, study the theory, run the simulation, and complete the posttest. Your recorded readings stay in this browser.

01Understand the Thévenin equivalent
02Explore load-power behaviour
03Verify the matching condition

Learning outcomes

  • State the condition for maximum power transfer in a DC network.
  • Calculate load current, voltage, power and efficiency for any load.
  • Explain why maximum power transfer gives only 50% efficiency.

Apparatus (virtual)

DC source (Vth), source resistance (Rth), variable load resistor (RL), ammeter, voltmeter.
