Experiment: Maximum Power Transfer Theorem

AimTheoryPretestProcedureSimulationPosttestLab reportReferencesContributorsFeedback

Aim

To verify the maximum power transfer theorem by finding the load resistance at which a DC source delivers the most power to the load, and to plot load power against load resistance.

Learning outcomes

  • State the condition for maximum power transfer in a DC network.
  • Calculate load current, voltage, power and efficiency for any load.
  • Explain why maximum power transfer gives only 50% efficiency.

Apparatus (virtual)

DC source (Vth), source resistance (Rth), variable load resistor (RL), ammeter, voltmeter.
