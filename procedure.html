Experiment: Maximum Power Transfer Theorem

Procedure

  1. Open the Simulation tab. In Part A, calculate Vth and Rth of the given network, check your answer, then send the values to Part B.
  2. Set the source voltage Vth and source resistance Rth using the sliders.
  3. Set the load resistance RL to a small value, such as 1 Ω.
  4. Read the current, load voltage, load power and efficiency, then click Record reading.
  5. Increase RL in steps and record at least 10 readings, including values below, near and above Rth.
  6. Click Show theoretical curve to compare your points with the calculated curve.
  7. Note the RL at which load power is highest and compare it with Rth.
  8. Change Rth and repeat to confirm the result holds for any source resistance.
